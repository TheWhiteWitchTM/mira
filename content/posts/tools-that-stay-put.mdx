Every tool on this site runs where you opened it. There is no account and no upload.

- **Unfurl** — see the link card on X, Bluesky, Discord, and Slack, then copy the tags.
- **Calling Card** — draw a 1200×630 or a square PNG with a name on it.
- **Post Meter** — paste a caption. See the X, Bluesky, LinkedIn, and Threads limits. Split a thread when you go over. X still counts a URL as 23 characters. Bluesky counts graphemes. People still ship a post that truncates, then act surprised.
- **Emoji, Flags, WebFont** — pick, compose, copy.
- **Image Magic** — background off, resize, icon set. The model runs in this tab.

I added Post Meter because I was tired of counting. Social networks do not agree on what a character is. The meter does.

Open [Tools](/tools/) and keep the tab. That is the whole product.
